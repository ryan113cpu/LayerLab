Choosing Your Material

The right material matters.

Different plastics have different strengths. The best choice depends on what your print needs to do.

PLA

A popular choice for decorative objects, prototypes, models and everyday accessories that do not need to withstand high temperatures.

Everyday projects

PETG

Often chosen for parts that need more toughness and flexibility than standard PLA prints.

Practical parts

TPU

A flexible material used for certain soft, bendable designs and protective accessories.

Flexible designs

Material availability depends on the printer and project. Printed parts are not automatically suitable for food contact, medical use or high-temperature applications.
